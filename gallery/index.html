Transitions between clips (scenes:)

10

On a scenes: entry, between each clip and the next; transition_duration sets how long. iris, flip, glitch, delaygrab and cairogradient also work on a single clip, as reveal:. melt/<service> takes any transition the installed melt has (melt -query transitions).

Wipe shapes (luma:)

12

With transition: wipe. Darkest parts change first (luma_reverse: true plays them lightest first); played here over the sample pictures, with a hard edge as by default. openshot/ ones need openshot-qt installed.

gl-transitions (transition: gl)

125

On a scenes: entry, transition: gl and gl: <name>; gl_params: {...} changes a transition's parameters (here, all at their defaults). Shaders from gl-transitions.com, run on the GPU.

All of melt's transitions (melt/)

58

Every transition the installed melt has, as transition: melt/<service> takes it, at its defaults (transition_properties: {...} sets the rest - melt -query transition=<service> lists them). Previews depend on melt and the samples only.

On the whole clip

16

Keys on a timeline clip (and, the framing and effect ones, on a scene's clip as {clip: ...} or {path: ...}). melt: takes any filter the installed melt has (melt -query filters).

Over time

13

Keys on a clip that change it as it plays: fades, keyframed motion and colour (position_keyframes, ken_burns_from/to, color_grade_keyframes), its speed, freezes.

Generated clips (derive:)

6

On a bin entry: a file made with ffmpeg from another one, before the render.

libopenshot effects (derive: openshot)

29

On a bin entry's derive: libopenshot's own effects, applied when the clip is generated - a picture one over the video sample, a sound one over the sample sound. Needs python3-openshot.

Titles

3

A bin entry of type: title, placed on a track above.

All of melt's video filters (melt:)

400

Every video filter the installed melt has - frei0r's, ffmpeg's (avfilter.*), MLT's own - as a clip's melt: list takes it, at its defaults; {<service>: {<property>: <value>}} sets them (melt -query filter=<service> lists them).

Analysis and debugging (melt:)

39

melt's filters that measure rather than change: scopes and graphs drawn over the picture (datascope shows the pixels' values, waveform and vectorscope their levels and colours), and detectors that only note what they find in the frame's metadata - for checking a video, not for the finished one.

melt's audio filters (melt:)

180

Every audio filter and transition the installed melt has: nothing to show, so only listed.
